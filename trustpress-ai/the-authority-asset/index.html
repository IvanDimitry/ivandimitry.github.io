The Authority Asset

How Experts Turn Raw Knowledge Into Books, Trust, and Market Memory.

Most experts do not have a knowledge problem. They have an architecture problem. This book shows how scattered expertise becomes structured trust: a book, executive edition, visual system, source page, launch ecosystem, and monthly authority engine.
