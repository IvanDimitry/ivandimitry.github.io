Glossary

The language of authority publishing.

Use this page to make the TrustPress AI category language clear, repeatable, and easy to cite.

Authority Asset

A source-backed, structured asset that turns expertise into something the market can trust, remember, share, and use.

Organized Trust

The result of turning raw knowledge into a clear thesis, proof, structure, voice, visuals, and distribution.

Market Memory

The mental association a buyer forms when they repeatedly connect your name, method, or category with a specific problem.

Proof Before Prose

A production rule: strong claims need proof, softer wording, or removal before drafting scales the argument.

Commercial Truth

The repeatable market belief that makes a book or authority asset commercially relevant.

Executive Edition

A compressed version of the book designed for busy decision makers and internal sharing.

Source-Backed Authority

Authority built from verifiable sources, lived experience, examples, claim discipline, and careful language.

Monthly Authority Engine

A recurring operating system for extracting signals, turning them into assets, distributing them, and refining based on response.

Trust Signal Dashboard

A way to track influence without pretending every outcome is directly attributable.

AI-Assisted Authority Publishing

A human-led production system where AI helps with speed, structure, extraction, and options while humans own truth, judgment, proof, and voice.

The Authority Asset is a TrustPress AI proof-of-concept by Ivan Dimitrijevic and Nataša Dimitrijevic.
