Authority Asset Scorecard

Diagnose the gap before you build the asset.

Use this quick scorecard to decide whether your raw knowledge is ready for a book, executive edition, visual system, authority audit, or monthly authority engine.

Is the core idea clear, specific, and worth building around?
Do you have credible evidence, examples, experience, or results?
Is there enough raw material to build from?
Is the point of view distinct, relatable, and ownable?
Can key ideas become useful diagrams, scorecards, or frameworks?
Do you have channels and a reason to repeat the idea?
Is this something the market needs to remember?
Does the asset naturally support a real offer or sales conversation?
